# FreelancerOS - Product Vision

## Vision

Build a modern all-in-one platform that enables freelancers and small businesses to manage clients, projects, invoices, tasks, finances, and productivity from a single dashboard.

---

## Mission

Freelancers often rely on multiple tools to run their business. FreelancerOS aims to simplify their workflow by providing one unified platform that reduces complexity, saves time, and improves productivity.

---

## Target Users

- Software Developers
- Designers
- Content Creators
- Digital Marketers
- Consultants
- Small Agencies
- Independent Professionals

---

## Problems We Solve

Freelancers commonly struggle with:

- Managing multiple clients
- Tracking project progress
- Creating professional invoices
- Monitoring payments
- Organising notes and documents
- Managing daily tasks
- Tracking working hours
- Monitoring business performance

---

## Product Goals

- Reduce the need for multiple applications
- Save freelancers time every day
- Improve organisation and productivity
- Provide clear business insights through analytics
- Deliver a clean and user-friendly experience

---

## Success Criteria

FreelancerOS will be considered successful when a freelancer can manage their daily business operations without relying on several separate tools.
