# FreelancerOS - Product Vision

## Vision

FreelancerOS is a modern platform that helps freelancers manage their entire business from one dashboard.

---

## Mission

Reduce the number of tools freelancers need by combining client management, project tracking, invoicing, notes and productivity into one application.

---

## Target Users

- Software Developers
- Designers
- Content Creators
- Agencies
- Consultants

---

## Problems We Solve

- Managing multiple clients
- Tracking projects
- Creating invoices
- Tracking payments
- Organizing notes
- Managing tasks

---

## Success

A freelancer can run their business using FreelancerOS without needing several different apps.
